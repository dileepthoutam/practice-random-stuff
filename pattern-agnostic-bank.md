# Pattern-Agnostic LeetCode Problem Bank

A large, deliberately mixed pool for independent practice, mock interviews, spaced review, and long-term reference. Problems are not grouped or tagged by topic, technique, or difficulty; the ordering is intentionally mixed so the title alone does not announce the intended approach.

This is a bank, not a completion target. Select a problem at random or have someone else choose one. First attempt it without consulting the weekly map or a solution. Before coding, state the input constraints, a baseline approach, the key observation, and how you will test it. Afterward, record whether you solved it independently and what needs review. Save advanced problems for when their prerequisites are familiar.

## How to use the bank

- Mix new and previously seen problems; do not follow the numbering as a syllabus.
- Choose problems across the range of challenge levels you have already studied. Avoid using an unseen advanced topic as a test of interview readiness.
- A problem solved with a solution-level hint is a learning attempt, not an independent solve. Put it back into the review rotation after a delay.
- Revisit misses after a few days and again in a later mixed session. Change a constraint or explain an alternate approach to check transfer.
- For mock rounds, randomly choose one or two prompts, set a realistic time limit, and reserve time to communicate, test, and review.
